[h1]Alien Invasion — War of the Worlds[/h1]
[i]No one would have believed, in the last years of the human age, that this city was being watched from the timeless worlds of space...[/i]

They came from the sky. A UFO mothership descends over your city, blasts it with lightning, and carves a smoking crater into the ground. Then the tripods come — striding across the land, firing heat-rays, and leaving a creeping red weed in their wake.

[h1]What it does[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Summon a UFO anywhere[/b] with the [b]UFO icon[/b] (in the Disasters panel) or the [b]F7[/b] key.
[*] The mothership [b]descends and bombards the ground with lightning[/b], then blows a disaster-scale [b]sinkhole crater[/b] and wrecks everything nearby.
[*] [b]Three tripods[/b] deploy from every UFO and [b]roam the land on their own[/b], firing [b]heat-ray beams[/b] that level buildings.
[*] A spreading [b]red “red weed” contamination[/b] is left behind, then fades over time.
[*] [b]Up to 5 invasions at once[/b] — stack them for total chaos.
[/list]

[h1]Details[/h1]
[list]
[*] Tripods stay for [b]14 in-game days[/b], then the mothership finally departs.
[*] Invasions can also strike [b]at random[/b] on their own.
[/list]

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[list]
[*] Open the [b]Disasters[/b] info-view panel and click the [b]UFO icon[/b] shown next to the vanilla disaster buttons (or press [b]F7[/b]).
[*] Click a spot on the map to summon the mothership there.
[*] Repeat to launch [b]up to 5[/b] invasions at the same time.
[/list]

[h1]Notes[/h1]
[list]
[*] The [b]UFO icon sits in the Disasters info-view panel[/b] (which requires the Natural Disasters DLC). Without that DLC, a UFO button appears at the top-right of the screen instead.
[*] This mod [b]destroys buildings and terrain[/b] — try it on a city you don't mind scarring, or keep a backup save.
[/list]

Welcome your new overlords. Have fun out there. 🛸

[h1]Language[/h1]
English and Japanese - the game's own language setting picks between them.
Other languages are welcome. The mod ships Locales/en.txt as a template: copy it to your language code (de.txt, fr.txt, zh.txt, ...), translate the values, and open a pull request on GitHub or post the file in the comments. Missing lines fall back to English, so a partial translation is fine.
